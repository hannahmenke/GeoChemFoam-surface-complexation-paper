\section{Conclusions}

This work presents a conservative Darcy-Brinkman-Stokes (DBS) formulation
\citep{soulaine2016micro} for PHREEQC-coupled reactive transport
\citep{parkhurst2013phreeqc,parkhurst2015phreeqcrm} with cell-based surface
complexation \citep{dzombak1990surface} in image-based porous media. The formulation defines the continuous model, the
discrete implementation, and the benchmark hierarchy used to assess the solver
across a range of analytical, image-based, and multi-scale porous-media cases.

The key contribution is a cell-based representation of unresolved reactive
surface area that is consistent with the DBS micro-continuum framework. By
using the physical mobile-water fraction $\theta$ as the accumulation and
chemistry storage variable, rather than the numerical DBS porosity, the method
preserves conservative aqueous transport and physically interpretable local
mass balances simultaneously. The unresolved micropore surface area is
introduced as an additive intrinsic volumetric density, allowing the method to
represent reactive uptake in image phases where the internal surface area
exceeds what can be recovered from the coarse-cell boundary alone.

The reported results demonstrate that the formulation: (i) reproduces the
dilute analytical retardation limit with sub-percent bulk inventory error;
(ii) produces physically interpretable dissolved and adsorbed fields in
transparent 2D and 3D image-based geometries; and (iii) remains computationally
tractable at application scale, with chemistry representing 36--55\% of the
total wall-clock time for the calcium surface-complexation system studied here.

The method closes a specific gap in existing DBS reactive transport capabilities
\citep{maes2021geochemfoam,maes2022vos}: conservative, unresolved surface
complexation in multi-scale image-based simulations, without requiring the
reactive surface to coincide with an explicitly resolved mesh boundary
\citep{soulaine2017mineral,molins2021simulation}. The natural extensions of
this work include Strang splitting \citep{strang1968construction} for reduced
operator-splitting error, validated surface-state restart, and multi-component
competitive surface complexation \citep{dzombak1990surface} in multi-ion
carbonate systems \citep{menke2015dynamic,menke2018fourd}.
