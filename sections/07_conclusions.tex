\section{Conclusions}

This work presents a conservative DBS formulation for PHREEQC-coupled reactive
transport with cell-based surface complexation. The manuscript defines the
continuous model, the discrete implementation, and the benchmark hierarchy
used to assess the final solver on analytical, image-based, and multiscale
porous-media cases.

The reported results include a quantitative 1D analytical retardation
benchmark, a corrected 2D fracture-network example with visible microporous
uptake, a 3D Kelvin case, and a corrected Estaillades-style production case
with reported chemistry metrics and a true 3D volume-rendered field figure.
Taken together, these examples show that the formulation can be reduced to
analytical limits and then applied to progressively more complex image-based
geometries while preserving the intended transport-reaction structure.

The remaining work is primarily editorial. The final manuscript still requires
complete literature citation and a final line-editing pass, but the governing
model, solver implementation, and benchmark set are now in place.
