\begin{abstract}
We present a diffuse-boundary-surface (DBS) formulation for PHREEQC-coupled
reactive transport with cell-based surface complexation in image-based porous
media. The formulation is designed to preserve conservative aqueous transport
in the physical mobile-water fraction, to maintain conservative local exchange
between dissolved and adsorbed inventories, and to avoid non-conservative
numerical corrections such as species clipping. The implementation is developed
through a staged sequence of verified reductions, culminating in a production
solver for DBS transport with optional unresolved surface complexation.

The manuscript is structured as a validation ladder that proceeds from a 1D
analytical retardation benchmark to a simple 2D image-based case, a simple 3D
image-based case, and a larger multi-scale 3D porous-medium demonstration. The
present manuscript includes a passing 1D analytical benchmark, a corrected 2D
fracture-network result with matrix-wide unresolved surface uptake, and 3D
volume-rendered field results for Kelvin and Estaillades-style test cases. At
this stage, the emphasis is placed on consolidating the mathematical statement,
the numerical implementation, and the benchmark evidence before final
literature references are added.
\end{abstract}
