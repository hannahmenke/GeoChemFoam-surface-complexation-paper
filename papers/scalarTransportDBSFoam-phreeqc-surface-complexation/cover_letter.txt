Dear Editor,

Please consider our research article, "A Conservative Darcy-Brinkman-Stokes
Formulation for PHREEQC-Coupled Reactive Transport with Cell-Based Surface
Complexation," by Hannah P. Menke, Florian Doster, and Julien Maes for
publication in Transport in Porous Media.

The manuscript presents a conservative Darcy-Brinkman-Stokes formulation that
couples reactive transport to PHREEQC-based surface complexation through a
cell-based representation of reactive surface area. This representation allows
surface reactions within segmented microporous phases while retaining the
resolved pore-space description of flow and transport. The transport and
chemical calculations use consistent cell water volumes and explicit dissolved
and surface-bound inventories.

The evidence reported includes a one-dimensional analytical retardation
benchmark, closed-system balance tests, controlled surface-area tests, and
image-based applications to fracture and limestone geometries. Together, these
results show how conservative coupling and cell-based reactive area can be
assessed without requiring every relevant mineral surface to be resolved by the
image or mesh. We expect the work to interest readers studying pore-scale
reactive transport, micro-continuum methods, contaminant retention,
critical-mineral separation, and radionuclide transport.

We confirm that this manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not
under consideration by another journal. All authors have approved the
manuscript and agree with its submission to Transport in Porous Media. The
authors declare that they have no relevant financial or non-financial interests
to disclose.
