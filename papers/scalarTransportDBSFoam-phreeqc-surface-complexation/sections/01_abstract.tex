\begin{abstract}
We present a Darcy-Brinkman-Stokes (DBS) micro-continuum formulation for PHREEQC-coupled
reactive transport with cell-based surface complexation in image-based porous
media. The formulation is designed to preserve conservative aqueous transport
in the physical mobile-water fraction, to maintain conservative local exchange
between dissolved and adsorbed inventories, and to avoid non-conservative
numerical corrections such as species clipping. The implementation is developed
through a staged sequence of verified reductions, yielding a production solver
for DBS transport with optional unresolved surface complexation.

The method is validated through a progressive benchmark hierarchy. A 1D
analytical retardation test confirms that the solver reproduces the field-level
retardation factor with a relative L1 profile error of $0.75\%$. A 2D
image-based fracture-network case demonstrates interpretable dissolved and
adsorbed calcium fields with matrix-wide unresolved surface uptake. Simple and
complex 3D image-based cases, based on Kelvin and Estaillades-style geometries,
show that the formulation extends to fully three-dimensional pore structures
while preserving the intended transport-reaction structure. Together, the
results establish the conservative DBS surface-complexation coupling as a
viable approach for multi-scale reactive transport in segmented porous-media
images.
\end{abstract}
