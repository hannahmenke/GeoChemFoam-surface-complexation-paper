\section{Numerical Implementation}

All chemistry-bearing quantities---aqueous species, $\epsilon$,
$a_{\mathrm{surf}}$, and surface species---are cell-centred; Darcy fluxes are
evaluated on faces. Time integration uses implicit Euler, advection uses
first-order upwind interpolation, and diffusion uses Gauss linear interpolation
with non-orthogonality correction. These choices give a bounded first-order
transport update consistent with the Lie split.

The area field is generated before transport from the segmented image and read
as a volume field. In quasi-two-dimensional cases, reconstructed contour length
is multiplied by the physical thickness; in three dimensions, reconstructed
triangle area is accumulated directly. Both paths yield the bulk area density
used by the same chemistry interface. Area is not recomputed during the flow or
reaction solve, so every PHREEQC surface retains the capacity established at
construction.

The mapped field is deliberately distinct from the numerical representation of
the velocity field. Darcy fluxes communicate aqueous mass between neighbouring
cells, whereas the area field has no face flux and is consulted only when the
local PHREEQC surface is constructed. A heterogeneous area distribution can
therefore alter equilibrium capacity without changing the prescribed hydraulic
properties. Conversely, modifying permeability or porosity does not imply a
change in surface area in the fixed-geometry calculations reported here.

The \texttt{phreeqcMixture} constructs one PHREEQC solution for every active
finite-volume cell and a surface state for every reactive cell. Cells with
$\epsilon<\texttt{epsChemistryMin}$ are excluded from the fixed mapping. The
site densities and $a_{\mathrm{surf}}$ establish the initial capacities; the
area and porosity fields remain fixed. At each timestep the solver transports
all aqueous species, clips negative inputs to zero subject to a fail-fast bound
on the relative mass added, runs local equilibrium chemistry, and returns both
aqueous and surface fields. OpenFOAM domain decomposition supplies process-level
parallelism, while each process uses one PhreeqcRM worker thread.

This ordering preserves an auditable interface between the two operators. The
transport solve advances $\theta C_i$ through face fluxes. One subsequent
chemistry call transfers all components and surface species, refreshes the
fixed solution volumes, executes \texttt{runCells()}, and copies the
equilibrated state back. Small negative values can arise from the transport
discretisation near a front; clipping prevents invalid PHREEQC inputs, while
the added-mass limit prevents clipping from silently becoming a material source.
Written-field diagnostics integrate cell volume times
$\theta C+a_{\mathrm{surf}}\Gamma$, so the same variables used by the solver
also define the reported conservation test.

The mapping is fixed for the duration of a calculation. This makes the number
of chemistry cells, their solution volumes, and their surface capacities
deterministic across timesteps and output times. It also defines the scope of
the implementation: evolving porosity, mineral surface, or chemistry-mask
topology would require rebuilding the PHREEQCRM mapping rather than simply
overwriting a finite-volume field. The present tests avoid that ambiguity by
holding these material fields fixed.

Complete output consists of the finite-volume fields and, when enabled, the
matching rank-local PHREEQCRM state. A completion marker is published only
after both state and field writes succeed. Restart must be requested explicitly
and fails if the matched checkpoint is absent or inconsistent; surface fields
alone do not reconstruct the complete chemical state. The detailed field
mapping, units, algorithm, checkpoint lifecycle, and reproducible commands are
documented in Online Resource~1.

The explicit restore switch prevents a stale dump from altering a fresh start.
Collective marker invalidation before an overwrite ensures that all ranks agree
that the previous state is no longer authentic. If a dump, field write, or
marker publication fails, the incomplete pair cannot be accepted on a later
restart. This lifecycle is required because PHREEQCRM carries internal chemical
state beyond the exported aqueous and \texttt{Surf\_*} fields.
